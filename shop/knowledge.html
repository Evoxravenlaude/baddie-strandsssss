Hair basics

01

Know Your Hair Type

From 1A to 4C, understanding your curl pattern, porosity and density is the first step. High-porosity hair absorbs moisture fast but loses it just as quickly. Low porosity needs warmth to open the cuticle.

02

Virgin vs Remy Hair

Virgin hair has never been chemically processed: it’s the purest form. Remy hair has all its cuticles aligned in one direction, which prevents tangling. All Baddie Strands hair is virgin and Remy.

03

Lace Types Explained

HD lace is the thinnest and most transparent, and disappears on every skin tone. Regular lace is thicker but more durable. 13×4 covers ear to ear, 13×6 adds depth for parting, and 360 lace covers the full perimeter.

04

Density Guide

Density is how full the wig looks. 150% is natural and light. 180% is full and glamorous. 200% is the maximum: dramatic, show-stopping volume. Choose for your lifestyle and the look you want.

05

Reading Hair Length

Hair is measured straightened. A 20″ straight wig reaches mid-back; a 20″ curly wig may look much shorter because of shrinkage. Always allow for your texture when choosing length.

06

Cap Construction

The cap is the foundation. Machine-made caps cost less but breathe less. Hand-tied caps move more naturally. Glueless caps have adjustable bands. Choose for comfort, lifestyle and activity level.
