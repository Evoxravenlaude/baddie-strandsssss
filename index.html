Our Story

Born in Abuja. Built for Every Queen.

Baddie Strands began with one mission: give Nigerian women access to the quality of hair they deserve, without compromise. We source directly from the finest suppliers, ensuring every strand is 100% virgin, ethically handled, and built to last.

We are a proudly Nigerian business — and our reach is worldwide. Whether you're in Wuse or Wimbledon, we bring the same uncompromising quality to your door. Born in Abuja, built for every queen on the globe — every package carries a piece of that Abuja excellence.
