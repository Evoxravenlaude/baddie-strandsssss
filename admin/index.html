How this works

A new order comes in

It shows on Overview and in Orders. Card payments arrive already marked Paid · preparing. Bank transfers arrive as Waiting for payment — check your Access Bank app, and once the money is there, open the order and tap Paid · preparing.

Sending an order out

When the rider picks it up, tap Out for delivery. When the customer has it, tap Delivered. Use the WhatsApp button on the order to message the customer in one tap.

Adding or changing a product

Go to Products → + Add product, or tap any product to edit it. Add a photo, name, price, lengths and colours, then Save. It appears on the shop straight away (shoppers may need to refresh).

Hiding a product that’s sold out

Switch On shop off on the product card. It stays here so you can switch it back on when it’s restocked. Or set stock to 0 — the shop then shows “Sold out”.

Sales and discounts

Edit the product and put the old price in Was (optional). The shop shows it crossed out next to the new price.

Changing the hair care text

Go to Hair care. Edit any title or text, add or remove steps, rename the sections, then tap Save changes. The shop’s Hair care page updates straight away.

Emails customers get

Customers get an email when they order by transfer (with your bank details), when payment is confirmed, when you tap Out for delivery, and when you tap Delivered. You get a copy of new and paid orders.

“Card payment not finished”

The customer opened the card payment but didn’t complete it. Message them on WhatsApp, or tap Cancel order if they paid another way.

Who can see this dashboard?

Only the shop email in config.js can sign in. Anyone else is turned away, and the database refuses to show them orders or customers.
